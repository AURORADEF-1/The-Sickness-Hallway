# Medical rework assets

- Clinic Door Leaf: 3D Assets, CC0 1.0. https://3dassets.dev/assets/hospital-wards-and-clinic-operations-door-leaf-90f1c7fd . Publisher identifies this as AI-generated geometry. Model modified with worn materials and collision setup.
- Crowbar: Clint Bellanger, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/crowbar . Model converted from Blender and scaled for first-person use. The staged lever animation is newly authored in this project, not a downloaded animation clip.
- Thornback: existing user-supplied mesh and maps, corrected skeleton landmarks/weights and procedural mottled skin shader. No new external creature licence assumed.

- Research service door: Breakable Doors and Windows by loafbrr_1, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/breakable-doors-and-windows . Original FBX, green base colour, normal, occlusion, metallic and roughness maps. Adapted to a fixed frame and separate hinged leaf, with matching crowbar contact and collision. Licence retained in Doors/LICENSE.txt.
