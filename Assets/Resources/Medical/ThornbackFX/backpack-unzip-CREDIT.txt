The source is a real zipper pull recording, tagged backpack, bag, open, opening and unzip. One complete pull was used. The public preview is the source, rather than the login-only original WAV.

Processing: mono 48 kHz PCM16, trim 0.030–0.875 seconds, DC removal, 8 ms fade-in / 25 ms fade-out, normalize to -5 dBFS peak. Final duration **0.845 seconds**. No generated/synthetic sound, time stretch, reverb or distortion baked in. `process.py` reproduces this from the retained decoded source.

Unity resource: `Medical/ThornbackFX/backpack-unzip`.

Timing recommendation: start about 0.45–0.65 seconds before the bag opens so the zipper's strongest action precedes the reveal and its quieter fabric tail bridges the opening.
